# LucerumOne Desktop — Release Repository

This is the **official download page** for **LucerumOne Desktop**.
It holds installers only. There is **no** source code here.

---

## About LucerumOne
LucerumOne is Lucerum's patient app. With it, patients complete the self-report surveys their clinician assigns, review their results over time, and manage their account and data-sharing preferences. Responses are sent to the clinician's Lucerum platform.

---

## Download
Every version is listed under [**Releases**](../../releases). Choose the newest one, open **Assets**, and download the installer for your computer:

| Platform | Installer |
|---|---|
| Windows | `.exe` setup or `.msi` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | `.dmg` |

Every installer is signed by Lucerum, and the macOS one is also notarized by Apple. If Windows says "Unknown publisher" or macOS refuses to open the app, do not install it. Contact us instead.
